Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Alkeme has no sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.

Can I buy or sell crypto in Alkeme?
No. Alkeme is a tracker only. It never connects to an exchange or wallet.

How often do prices update?
About once a minute while the app is open. Pull down on any list to refresh. Prices come from CoinGecko and may be slightly delayed.

When do price alerts fire?
Alkeme checks your alerts each time prices refresh while the app is open, and sends a notification when a target is reached. Make sure notifications for Alkeme are on in iOS Settings.

Prices say "busy" or "no connection".
The free market-data service limits how many requests one connection can make per minute. Wait a moment and pull to refresh; your last prices stay on screen.

How do I delete my data?
Open Alkeme → the gear icon → Delete all data on this device. Deleting the app also removes everything.
