A private crypto portfolio tracker for iPhone. Follow live prices for the top 250 coins, track what you hold and your profit or loss, set price alerts, and see the whole market at a glance as a bubble chart or heatmap.

No account. No tracking. Your portfolio, alerts and watchlist are stored only on your phone. Alkeme has no server of its own and collects no personal data.

Alkeme is a tracker. It is not an exchange, broker, wallet or adviser: it cannot buy, sell or hold crypto, and nothing in it is financial advice.

Market data: CoinGecko. Fear & Greed Index: Alternative.me.
