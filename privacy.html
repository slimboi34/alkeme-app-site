Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers the Alkeme iPhone app ("the app").

The short version

Alkeme does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

The coins you hold, their amounts and buy prices, your price alerts and your watchlist are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. They are deleted if you use About → Delete all data on this device or uninstall the app.

Market data requests

To show prices, charts and coin descriptions, the app requests public market data directly from CoinGecko, and the Crypto Fear & Greed Index from Alternative.me. These requests contain only which coins or charts are wanted. They do not include your holdings, alerts or any identifier. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to those services, which handle it under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, price alerts are delivered as local notifications generated on your device. No push token is created or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
